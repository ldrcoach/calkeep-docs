# CalKeep Documentation

Welcome. CalKeep is one product for calendars, contacts, bookings, tasks, processes, and pipeline — built for solo professionals and small teams.

These docs are for **workspace admins and end users** who want to set up CalKeep, connect calendars, onboard a team, and run day-to-day work.

## Where to start

- **[Getting started](/getting-started)** — what to do in your first 10 minutes after signing up. Best for new admins who just activated a workspace.
- **[Connect accounts and sources](/integrations/google-calendar)** — choose the Google or Microsoft Calendars, Contacts, and Tasks you want CalKeep to request, then review each discovered source before enabling it.
- **[Admin Hub](/admin/admin-hub)** — orient workspace administrators, invite teammates, coordinate delegation, configure security, and connect services without treating availability as configured health.
- **[FAQ](/faq)** — quick answers to the most-asked questions.

## Platform and support topics

- **[Platform automation](/admin/platform-automation)** - API tokens, signed webhooks, CSV exports, and guarded import.
- **[Team visibility and privacy](/admin/team-calendar-visibility)** - coordinate availability without exposing every private calendar detail.
- **[FAQ](/faq)** - activation, cancellation, seat quantity, support, data export, and workspace deletion questions.

## What CalKeep does

| Capability | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| **Calendar connections** | Review and enable supported provider calendars in one unified view; new iCloud and Yahoo app-password onboarding is currently paused |
| **Booking pages** | Public scheduling links — like Calendly, but with your branding |
| **Contacts (CRM)** | Review provider contact books before enabling sync, dedupe the CalKeep copy, share with teammates, and manage follow-up reminders |
| **Tasks & projects** | Inbox + project boards + recurring tasks, with calendar drag-to-schedule |
| **Processes** | Templated workflows that auto-spawn tasks on a schedule |
| **Branded experience** | Your logo, colors, and copy on every customer-facing surface |
